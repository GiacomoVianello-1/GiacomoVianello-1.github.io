Giacomo Vianello

Hi, I am Giacomo! I am a Master's student in Control Systems Engineering at the University of Padua, specializing in robotics and intelligent systems.

Currently, I am an intern at the University of Zaragoza within the RoPeRT group (I3A). My research focuses on the integration of Artificial Intelligence and Aerial Vehicles for my Master's thesis, under the guidance of Prof. Ruggero Carli and the supervision of Prof. Eduardo Montijano and David M. Cabello.

Prior to this, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering from the University of Padua, graduating with a perfect score of 110/110.

I am always open to discussing new ideas, research, or potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
