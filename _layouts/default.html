Giacomo Vianello

Hi, I am Giacomo! I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Information Engineering at the University of Padua, advised by Prof. Ruggero Carli. My research interests lie at the intersection of autonomous robotics, control systems, and artificial intelligence.

In early 2026, I was a visiting research intern at the University of Zaragoza within the RoPeRT group, part of the Aragon Institute of Engineering Research (I3A). During my visit, I conducted my Master's thesis research, titled "Spatial Intelligence with Vision-Language Models Onboard Drones", under the supervision of Prof. Eduardo Montijano and David M. Cabello. The project focused on integrating lightweight Vision-Language Models (VLMs) onboard aerial vehicles to enable autonomous, natural-language-driven, goal-oriented navigation.

I received my M.Sc. in Control Systems Engineering in September 2026 (110/110 cum laude) and my B.Sc. in Electronic Engineering (110/110) in July 2024, both from the University of Padua.

I am always open to discussing new ideas, research, or potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
