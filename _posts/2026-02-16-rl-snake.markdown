abstract: "A comparative study of Deep Reinforcement Learning algorithms applied to the classic Snake game across fully and partially observable environments. Implemented in PyTorch with vectorized parallel rollouts, the benchmark evaluates Double DQN (DDQN), Advantage Actor-Critic (A2C), and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) against a heuristic baseline. Through shaped reward formulations and action masking, PPO demonstrates superior sample efficiency, training stability, and survival time, outperforming value-based approaches in complex state regimes."
