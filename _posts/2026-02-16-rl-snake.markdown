In this project, I developed a custom environment for the Snake game to test different **Deep Reinforcement Learning** algorithms. I compared policy-gradient methods like **PPO** and **A2C** against value-based methods like **Double DQN**. A significant part of the work involved designing the state representation for partially observable scenarios, where the agent only sees a limited 3x3 or 5x5 grid around its head.
