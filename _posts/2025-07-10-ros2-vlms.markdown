Prior to my internship at the **University of Zaragoza**, I developed this ROS2-based infrastructure to bridge the gap between high-level semantic reasoning and robotic platforms. The system is organized into two main packages: `vlm_api_package`, which interfaces with the **Gemini API** for powerful cloud-based reasoning, and `vlm_local_package`, which implements local inference using **SmolVLM** and **BLIP** for privacy and edge computing scenarios. 

The framework allows a drone to publish its camera stream and receive semantic descriptions of the environment, which can then be used for complex tasks like autonomous path planning and human-robot interaction.
